DRIVER BRIEFING

How to play

Drive

WASD or arrow keys. On mobile, use the touch pedals and steering arrows. Tap GAS to launch.

Drift

Hold SPACE or the handbrake while turning, then feed in the throttle. Higher drift stat keeps the slide composed.

Explore

Open the map with M, fast travel to any discovered region and hunt the 24 golden gears hidden across the island.

Race

Drive into a glowing event gate and press E, or launch from the map. Beat the target time for gold and extra credits.

Collect & tune

Earn credits to buy 30 cars. In the garage, change paint, rims and upgrades. Every car has its own physics and engine note.

Camera

C cycles chase, near, hood, cockpit and cinematic cameras. R resets the car. H shows this briefing.
